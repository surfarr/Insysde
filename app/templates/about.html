About

Campus Dashboard shows weather, next bus departures and commercial aircraft overhead for the University of Liechtenstein campus in Vaduz. Data is cached for 60 seconds. Built for the Information Systems Development course.

Data sources

  • Open-Meteo – weather, licensed CC BY 4.0.
  • transport.opendata.ch – public transport departures.
  • airplanes.live – ADS-B aircraft positions.

All free for non-commercial use, without any service guarantee.
